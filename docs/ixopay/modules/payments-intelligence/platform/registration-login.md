# Registration & Login
To create a demo account, you would need to contact the IXOPAY team:
  1. Go to our [Demo Request page](https://app.congrify.com/sign-up), provide your contact details and information on your needs and click Submit to sign-up. Our team will shortly reach out to you to schedule a short demo session, and verify your request via an email sign-up link.
  2. Check your inbox for the Email Verification, then click on “confirm email”.
**Important:** Check your spam folder if you are not finding the email in your main inbox.
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/RegistrationLogin_2.e3c9cdc.1600.png)
  3. Fill in your information details and create a password to proceed with the sign up.
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/RegistrationLogin_3.76fc0bb.1600.png)
  4. Your account has now been created. To access the platform, click on “proceed to your account”.
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/RegistrationLogin_4.a0148d7.1600.png)
  5. You now have access to the demo app dashboard and are able to navigate through the different features and functionalities.
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/RegistrationLogin_5.ed96739.1600.png)
If you face any technical difficulties, please contact the support team.
