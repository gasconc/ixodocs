# IXO AI
IXOPAY Payments Intelligence's (formerly Congrify) IXO AI is a dedicated payments intelligence tool designed to transform complex, fragmented data into clear, actionable insights. Unlike traditional business intelligence tools that require manual data wrangling and advanced querying skills, IXO AI acts as an automated payments analyst. It works in the background to monitor specific patterns and user-defined KPIs, enabling teams to uncover anomalies in authorization rates, identify unexpected spikes in gateway or interchange fees, and explore your enriched and normalized payments operations data in real-time.
## How to Start[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#how-to-start "Direct link to How to Start")
Getting started with IXOPAY Payments Intelligence’s advanced features follows a unified process to ensure your data is correctly mapped and ready for analysis.
  1. **Core Integration:** Before enabling IXO AI, you must connect your payment data sources. For a step-by-step guide on connecting your PSPs, please refer to the [Integrations Getting Started Guide](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/integrations/getting-started).
  2. **No-Code Activation:** Once your integrations are live, IXO AI automatically is ready to assist you with accurate payments insights. There is no additional technical setup required to begin using the conversational interface.
  3. **Data Enrichment:** For deeper intelligence, you can provide supplemental business context via secure file sharing or API. Please contact the support team to see how to share the data.

To start using the IXO AI, click on the "Ask IXO" button on the top-left corner of your screen (i.e. side-bar panel).
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/IXOAI_1.17bff7e.1600.png)
**IMPORTANT:** Depending on the plan that is active to your users, IXO AI interactions may have different usage thresholds. If you hit your interaction thresholds, please contact support or reach out to your point of contact.
## How to use IXO AI[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#how-to-use-ixo-ai "Direct link to How to use IXO AI")
IXO AI uses an intuitive conversational interface, allowing you to bypass complex dashboards and ask direct questions about your payment performance, payments industry knowledge and the IXOPAY Payments Intelligence technical documentation and user-guides.
  * **Ask Conversational Questions:** Instead of building tables and visualizations manually, you can ask questions like: "What is my current authorization rate?" or "Has there been a decline spike in the last 24 hours?"
  * **Drill Down Into Root Causes:** If an issue is identified, follow up with granular queries such as: "Which combination of payment method and billing country is affected?"
  * **Identify Cost Spikes:** Use IXO AI to monitor financial efficiency by asking it to spot unusual patterns in scheme, interchange, or gateway fees. Simultaneously, you can get a full picture of your fee with the applied fee allocation rules, on a transaction-event level.

IXO AI responds with detailed insights and visual summaries, based on your data, enabling you to catch payment issues early and suggest next steps - so you can intuitively explore root-causes and impacts, and adjust your management strategy before such issues escalate into customer service problems.

## IXO AI functionalities[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#ixo-ai-functionalities "Direct link to IXO AI functionalities")
### Scheduled Analysis[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#scheduled-analysis "Direct link to Scheduled Analysis")
In the IXO AI chat interface, navigate to the IXO AI Chat side-panel and click on the "Scheduled" button to view your historical scheduled analysis, create new and edit existing scheduled analysis from IXO AI.
To create a new scheduled analysis, you can define the title, prompt (define the IXO AI natural language prompt), frequency (schedule time) and notification preferences. Post creation, you can also trigger a scheduled analysis execution from IXO AI, independent of the next scheduled run.
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/IXOAI_2.ccabf3a.1600.png)
Existing scheduled analysis, and historical analysis results, can be viewed directly in the chat interface. Further you can edit (similar to creation step above), pause and delete the existing scheduled analysis. You can further deep-dive into the analysis results by clicking on "Start new chat from this answer", which will re-direct you to a new IXO AI Chat interface with context of the scheduled analysis results to continue.
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/IXOAI_3.e7b74aa.1600.png)
### Prompt Library[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#prompt-library "Direct link to Prompt Library")
The Prompt Library is the central place to store, organize and reuse the questions you ask IXO. Launch a proven prompt with one click, save your best ones, tag them by topic and share them with colleagues so everyone works from the same questions.
#### How to Start[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#how-to-start-1 "Direct link to How to Start")
  1. In the left navigation, click **Ask IXO**.
  2. In the **IXO AI** panel, click **Library**.
  3. Choose a tab: **Recommended** , **My prompts** or **Shared with me**.

No additional setup is required. The Library uses the data sources already connected to IXO.
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/IXOAI_4.79a94c6.1600.png)
#### Tabs[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#tabs "Direct link to Tabs")
  * **Recommended:** Ready-to-use prompts curated by IXOPAY, tagged **Default** plus a topic (**Sales** , **Chargeback** , **Fees** or **Performance**). Read-only: they cannot be edited, shared or deleted.
  * **My prompts:** Prompts you created. Only you can see them until you share them.
  * **Shared with me:** Prompts colleagues in your company shared with you.

Each prompt card shows the prompt text, its tags and creation date. Each tab shows the number of results. Use **Search prompts** to filter the selected tab. To run a prompt, click **Start a new chat** : IXO opens a new chat and answers using your latest data.
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/IXOAI_5.016bb22.1600.png)
#### Create a prompt[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#create-a-prompt "Direct link to Create a prompt")
  1. Open **My prompts** and click **Add prompt**.
  2. Enter your question in the **Prompt** field (mandatory).
  3. Optional: click **Add tag** , select an existing tag (Authorization Rates, Chargeback, Default, Fees, Performance, Sales) or type a new name in **Search or create a tag**. You can add multiple tags.
  4. Optional: select **Share with team** to share the prompt with your company.
  5. Click **Save**. The prompt appears in **My prompts**.

![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/IXOAI_6.8363d72.1600.png)
#### Edit, share or delete a prompt[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#edit-share-or-delete-a-prompt "Direct link to Edit, share or delete a prompt")
In **My prompts** , click the three dots on the prompt card and select **Edit** , **Share...** or **Delete**.
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/IXOAI_7.9ae2c43.1600.png)
#### Share a prompt[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#share-a-prompt "Direct link to Share a prompt")
  1. Click the three dots and select **Share...**. The **Share Prompt** window lists **People with access** ; you are the **Owner**.
  2. To share with specific colleagues, click **Add people...** and select a user.
  3. To share with your whole company, open the menu next to **Anyone in your company** and choose an access level:
     * **Can view** (default): see and run the prompt.
     * **Can edit:** see, run and edit the prompt.
     * **No access:** the prompt stays private.
  4. Click **Share**.

![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/IXOAI_8.ebe723a.1600.png)
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/IXOAI_9.aa1c4bc.1600.png)
note
You remain the Owner of every prompt you share. To stop sharing, set **Anyone in your company** to **No access** and remove any added people.
#### View prompts shared with you[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#view-prompts-shared-with-you "Direct link to View prompts shared with you")
Open **Shared with me** to see prompts your colleagues shared. Until then, the tab shows "Nothing shared with you yet".
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/IXOAI_10.9bb2ddd.1600.png)

### View suggested prompts basis your exploration[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#view-suggested-prompts-basis-your-exploration "Direct link to View suggested prompts basis your exploration")
IXO AI will suggest next recommended prompts to further explore your payments data keeping the context from your chat. This enables an intuitive exploration of your payments data to go from inquiry to root cause analysis to obtaining insightful recommendations to optimize your payments operations with minimal input.
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/IXOAI_19.5077bdc.1600.png)
### Access the IXO AI Analysis within Analytics[​](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/docs/payments-intelligence/observability/ai-copilot#access-the-ixo-ai-analysis-within-analytics "Direct link to Access the IXO AI Analysis within Analytics")
You can access the IXO AI functionality within the Analytics dashboard. Simply click on the preferred dimension’s table you want to analyse, and then click on the “Ask IXO” button. This would create a dual-pane view with the IXO AI analysis of the defined KPI-dimension table in Analytics, which can be further explored and refined basis the Analytics widgets/ tables and the IXO AI chat.
To move from the Analytics dual-pane view to the IXO AI chat interface, click on “convert to IXO AI chat”.
![](https://documentation.ixopay.com/modules/assets/ideal-img/IXOAI_20.eb2fa92.1600.png)

To enable the Slack connection - please reach out to your IXOPAY Payments Intelligence Support team. Once granted, you can enable IXO AI (Beta) in Slack by enabling the connection in the Merchant Settings on the IXOPAY Payments Intelligence platform by the Merchant Admin. With the IXO AI-Slack app integration, you can interact with your IXO AI instance by prompting with @IXOAI as pre-fix, get IXO AI responses in the reply-thread and further tag your allowed colleagues in the group chat or further deep-dive with IXO AI.
